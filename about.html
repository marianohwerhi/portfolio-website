About

A nurse who learned to build the dashboard herself

Most healthcare dashboards are built by people who have never worked a shift on a ward. I have, across CAMHS, acute mental health, rehabilitation, and crisis services, and that's exactly what shapes how I model data, choose KPIs, and decide what a clinical team actually needs to see first.

I trained as a Registered Mental Health Nurse and moved into healthcare analytics after noticing the same gap repeatedly from the ward side. Clinical teams generate huge amounts of data and rarely get it back in a form that helps them make faster, safer decisions. That's the problem I now build dashboards to solve, using Power BI, SQL, Excel, and Tableau, with a clinical governance and safeguarding background behind it, so "handle this data responsibly" means something concrete in practice, not just in theory.
